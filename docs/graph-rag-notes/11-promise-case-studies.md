# 11 · RQ2 case studies: ten commitments followed through the reports

Started 2026-10-03. The supervisor asked for fulfilled and unfulfilled promises shown as cases with
evidence (`08-promise-fulfilment-market-screen.md`). `10-promise-trajectories.md` gives the counts; this
note gives the cases, each as a timeline of the company's own sentences.

Reading rules for everything below:

- **Citations** are `report year p.N`. The year is the report's year in the store (`reports.year`); `N`
  is the page position in the PDF as parsed (`statements.page`), which can differ from the printed page
  number (Kim Heng 2022 PDF page 17 is printed page 15). Source files are in `reports.file`, e.g.
  `raw/indofood-agri/2024_2e075abc.pdf`.
- **Quotes are verbatim** from the parsed statements, with three repairs: broken ligature glyphs are
  normalised ("consumpƟon" → "consumption"), footnote digits fused to a word are dropped
  ("incidents13" → "incidents"), and in a quote from a flattened table " / " marks a cell boundary the
  parse lost.
- **A case says what the reports say.** None of them is a finding of greenwashing. A postponement can
  have a good reason, and a commitment that is not mentioned again may still have been delivered.

## 1. How the cases were chosen

Pool: the 74 human-checked promises of `10` §5–6, verdict rows first (achieved 14, failed 8, revised 6,
disappeared 11). Criteria: the commitment names a date and something checkable; the outcome sentence
comes from the same company's later reports, with no outside source; one case per pattern; at most two
threads per company; small and large issuers both present.

Every chosen thread was then re-read against **all** statements of that company in the store, every
report year, by keyword search on the commitment's own nouns (patterns in §4), instead of the six
retrieved statements the check sheet showed. That step changed the reading of five of the ten threads
(§3), which is itself a result.

### Case 1 · Tiong Woon — solar panels by FY2023 (delivered)

| report | sentence |
|---|---|
| 2022 p.26 | "we are planning to install solar panels in our recently acquired premises at 12 Benoi Place, and the project is expected to complete in FY2023." |
| 2022 p.27 | target table: "Install solar panels within the workplace premises by FY2023 / In progress with target completion date in FY2023" |
| 2023 p.33 | "we had installed solar photovoltaic (PV) panels at the location of 12 Benoi Place." |
| 2024 p.33 | "In FY2024, 155,700 kWh of electricity from the solar panels was directed for use at the site, resulting in an offset of 64 tonnes of CO2e" |
| 2025 p.38 | "In FY2025, 76,575 kWh of electricity from the solar panels was directed for use at the site, resulting in an offset of 31.55 tCO2e of GHG emissions." |

A milestone with a place and a date, reported as done in the deadline year, and followed by output
figures afterwards. This is what a closed promise looks like in the corpus.

### Case 2 · Indofood Agri — allergen disclosure (one point short, delivered a year late)

| report | sentence |
|---|---|
| 2021 p.48–49 | 50 % of domestic margarine and shortening packaging carried allergen information; "We are aiming for a 100% allergen disclosure rate by the end of 2022." |
| 2022 p.56 | "In 2022, 99% of our domestic margarine and shortening packaging contained allergen information and 100% had been registered for packaging design changes." "We expect to achieve a 100% allergen disclosure rate by mid-2023." |
| 2023 p.63 | "As of 2023, we have achieved a 100% allergen disclosure rate on our domestic margarine and shortening packaging." |

Missed at the deadline by the company's own figure, a new date stated in the same report, and that date
met. The sheet label `failed` is right for the 2022 deadline and would mislead if read alone.

### Case 3 · Gallant Venture — GHG −10 % against 2017 (deadline moved, level met)

| report | sentence |
|---|---|
| 2020 p.14 | "Our GHG emissions target is to decrease emissions by 10% by 2022 (baseline 2017)." |
| 2021 p.14 | "Our GHG emissions target in the medium term is to decrease emissions by 10% by 2024 (baseline 2017)." Table on the same page: Scope 1 12,367 tCO2e (2020), 9,916 tCO2e (2021). |
| 2022 p.14 | "reduce our GHG emissions by 10% from our 2017 baseline year by 2024." "In 2022, we obtained 93% efficiency rate and achieved a 15.4% reduction in GHG emissions from 2017 (17,962.0 tCO2e)." |
| 2023 p.14 | "FY2023 Target : 10% reduction from 2017 baseline of 17,962 tCO2e FY2023 Achievement : 14,284 tCO2e (20% reduction of 2017 baseline) FY2024 Target : 12% reduction from 2017 baseline of 17,962 tCO2e" |
| 2024 p.14 | "FY2024 Achievement : 13,649 tCO2e (24% reduction from 2017 baseline) FY2025 Target : 12% reduction from 2017 baseline of 17,962 tCO2e" |
| 2025 p.14 | "In FY2025, emissions were recorded at 9,791 tCO2e, representing a 46% reduction from the baseline, exceeding the target." |

Three things are visible only across years. The deadline changes from 2022 to 2024 between two reports,
and no neighbouring sentence in the 2021 report mentions the change. The level was nevertheless reported
as met at the original deadline (15.4 % in 2022). And from FY2023 the yearly target (10 %, then 12 %)
sits below the reduction the same table reports as already achieved (20 %, 24 %); if the 2020 and 2021
Scope 1 figures share the baseline's boundary, they were already 31 % and 45 % below it when the 10 %
target was stated. The promise is kept, and it was not a demanding one.

### Case 4 · Indofood Agri — 100 % ISPO certification (moved twice, still open)

| report | target as stated | progress in the same report |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 p.13, p.16 | "we aim to have 100% of our mills and estates ISPO certified by 2023." | 80 % of targeted hectarage; 18 of 27 mills |
| 2021 p.11, p.30 | same sentence, by 2023 | 83 %; 18 of 27 mills |
| 2022 p.5, p.34 | "on track to achieve our goals of ISPO certification for all nucleus estates and mills by 2024, which has been extended by 1 year due to additional new planting." | 86 %; 20 of 27 mills |
| 2023 p.6, p.40 | "We are on track to achieve our goals of ISPO certification for all nucleus estates and mills by the end of 2024." | 89 %; 21 of 27 mills |
| 2024 p.18, p.43 | "Our target to achieve ISPO certification across our operations by 2024 has been postponed due to new government regulations." "… achieving 100% ISPO certification across our operations by the end of 2026." | 89 %; 22 of 27 mills |
| 2025 p.18, p.45 | "We are committing to achieve 100% ISPO certification, in alignment with the Government Regulations." | 89 %; 22 of 27 mills |

Both postponements are disclosed with a reason, which is the opposite of a silent change. What the
timeline adds: "on track" was stated one year before the second miss, at 89 % and 21 of 27 mills;
certified hectarage has stood at 89 % for three reports (the figures count sites that have completed
the first-stage audit, per the reports' own footnote); and the 2025 goals table lists the same two
ISPO goals "(in line with government regulations)" without the "By end 2026" its 2024 version carried,
so the parsed 2025 report contains no dated ISPO target. The scope wording also narrows from "estates"
to "nucleus estates" in 2022.

### Case 5 · Gallant Venture — Scope 3 reporting by FY2023 (the date dissolves)

| report | sentence |
|---|---|
| 2022 p.34 | "We will be expanding our reporting scope to include Scope 3 categories by FY2023." |
| 2023 p.26 | "We are looking into incorporating several Scope 3 GHG emission categories into our subsequent reports." |
| 2024 p.27 | "We are exploring including several Scope 3 GHG emission categories in our future reports." |
| 2025 p.27 | "The Group is also exploring the inclusion of selected Scope 3 GHG emission categories in future disclosures." |

The verb weakens every year ("will" → "looking into" → "exploring" → "exploring") and the date is never
replaced. No report says that the FY2023 date passed. Each later sentence, read alone, is an
unremarkable statement of intent; only the sequence shows a commitment that was dated once.

### Case 6 · CDL HTrust — science-based target (milestone slips a year per report)

| report | sentence |
|---|---|
| 2023 p.21 | "we intend to derive near, medium, and long term emissions reduction targets by undertaking a target-setting exercise aligned with SBTi in FY 2024." |
| 2024 p.7 | targets table, "Targets for FY 2025": "Decarbonisation target validated with SBTi" |
| 2024 p.23 | "We have begun the process and aim to get this target validated by 2025." "CDLHT will also explore deriving our short-, medium-, and long-term decarbonisation targets … through the target-setting approach under SBTi." |

### Case 7 · Old Chang Kee — yearly electricity target (misses stated plainly)

The target is renewed each year: electricity per square foot not above the previous year's.

| FY | kWh per sq ft | the report's own verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 68 (FY2019: 68) | target set for FY2021 (2020 p.14) |
| 2021 | 61 | none stated; the figure meets it (2021 p.14) |
| 2022 | 69 | "We did not meet our FY2022 target … due to the higher retail sales in FY2022 as a result of the gradual loosening of Covid-19 safe management measures compared to FY2021." (2022 p.14) |
| 2023 | 66 | "We had met our FY2023 target" (2023 p.14) |
| 2024 | 68.0 | "Requires improvement as we recorded an electricity consumption (kWh) per square foot of 68.0, a slight increase from FY2023." (2024 p.8) |
| 2025 | not found | the parsed report gives combined energy intensity instead: "86.55 kWh (2.3% lower than 2024) per square foot, which includes both fuelbased and electricity consumption." (2025 p.9) |

The company treats safety the same way: "We have achieved the accident frequency target of zero for
FY2023, and will endeavour to continue meeting this target in FY2024" (2023 p.27), then "Requires

find because the company says it. In FY2025 the indicator changes from electricity to all energy, so the
series cannot be continued from the parsed text.

## 3. What reading the whole timeline changed

The check sheet asked for a judgement "from the evidence shown", six statements from the deadline-year
reports. Those labels are right under that rule. Reading every statement of the company gives a
different answer for five of the ten threads:

| thread | sheet (six statements) | after the full search |
|---|---|---|
| Indofood Agri ISPO, 2024 deadline (P00323) | failed | missed and explicitly postponed to 2026 (2024 p.9, p.18) |
| Gallant Venture GHG (P00268) | revised, original not reported as met | deadline moved, and 15.4 % reported for 2022 (2022 p.14) |
| Old Chang Kee electricity FY2021 (P00461) | unclear | 61 against 68 kWh per sq ft (2021 p.14): met by the figure |
| Old Chang Kee accident frequency FY2024 (P00472) | disappeared | two cases, "Requires improvement" (2024 p.9) |
| ThaiBev solar rooftops (P00761) | disappeared | two of four sites reported (2023 p.169) |

The same search was then run for **all 11 rows the check labelled `disappeared`**:

| promise | found in the reports? |
|---|---|
| P00761 ThaiBev solar rooftops, 2023 | yes: partial delivery (2023 p.169) |
| P00472 Old Chang Kee accident frequency, FY2024 | yes: missed (2024 p.9) |
| P00347 Jumbo complaints handled under protocol, FY2022 | yes: the FY2022 performance table lists it with "Yes" (2022 p.15) |
| P00329 Indofood Agri recyclable pouch, 2025 | yes, but two years early: "our original hope of a viable product by 2025 has been delayed" (2023 p.63) |
| P00811 Tiong Woon ERP launch, first half of 2022 | yes: the system is described as in use in FY2022, no launch date (2022 p.17) |
| P00407 LHN digital name cards, FY2022 | no |
| P00808 Thakral collaboration with Polygon, 2024 | no ("Polygon" does not occur after 2023) |
| P00371 Kim Heng formalised P&P, 2024 | no (later reports mention only the sustainability policies) |
| P00428 MediLifestyle antibody test kit sales, 2022 | no (later reports mention only the supplier's brain trauma test kit) |
| P00760 ThaiBev training in Buriram and Chiang Rai, 2023 | no |
| P00723 ThaiBev 150 beverage providers attending, 2021 | no |

**5 of 11 are in the reports after all.** Taken at face value the `disappeared` share of `10` §6 (5.3 %)
would fall to roughly 3 %; n is 11, the search was done by one person who knew the labels, so this is a
direction, not a corrected estimate. The three causes are distinct:

1. **Top-6 retrieval in a long report.** ThaiBev's 2023 report has 1,976 statements; the right sentence
   was not among the six.
2. **Flattened table rows.** Old Chang Kee 2024 and Jumbo 2022 report results in target tables, which
   parse into one long line and rank low against a prose query.
3. **The evidence window.** Evidence is drawn from the deadline-year report and the year after. Indofood
   Agri disclosed the delay of a 2025 target in its 2023 report, outside that window.

For the system this points one way: a commitment's outcome needs the statements about its metric from
every report year of the company, in order, which is a join on (company, metric, year) and not a top-k
similarity search in one year. Not built yet; it is the next concrete step for the graph line.

## 4. Reproducing the timelines

Each timeline is a case-insensitive regex over one company's statements, all years:

```python
import duckdb, re
c = duckdb.connect("artifacts/graph/dev/graph.duckdb", read_only=True)
rows = c.execute("select year, page, text from statements where company = ? order by year, page, idx",
                 [company]).fetchall()
hits = [(y, p, t) for y, p, t in rows if re.search(pattern, t, re.I)]
```

```python
PATTERNS = [  # (case, company, pattern)
    (1, "Tiong Woon", r"solar"),
    (2, "Indofood Agri", r"allergen"),
    (3, "Gallant Venture", r"2017 baseline|baseline 2017|from 2017"),
    (4, "Indofood Agri", r"By end 202\d: ISPO|ISPO.{0,80}(by|end) 202[3-6]|ISPO.{0,60}(postponed|extended)|100% ISPO"),
    (5, "Gallant Venture", r"scope 3"),
    (6, "CDL HTrust", r"SBTi|science[- ]based|validat"),
    (7, "Old Chang Kee", r"per square f|did not meet|requires improvement|accident frequency"),
    (8, "Kim Heng", r"corruption.*(zero|incident|case)|suspected|lost[- ]time"),
    (9, "ThaiBev", r"solar rooftop.*distribution|distribution cent.*solar"),
    (10, "LHN", r"name ?card|business card"),
]
```

## 5. Use and limits

- **Assistive screening, not a verdict.** The cases show where a reader should look and what the reports
  say there. Whether a postponement was justified, or whether an unmentioned pledge was quietly kept,
  is outside what the text can settle.
- **Chosen, not sampled.** The ten threads illustrate patterns. Prevalence is in `10` §6.
- **Parsed text only.** Figures inside images are not seen; flattened tables lose their column
  structure; "not found" means not found in the parsed statements with the patterns above.
- **One reader.** The search and the reading were done by one person with the sheet labels in view.
- **For the market screen (`08`).** The dated events these cases yield are disclosures: the report in
  which a miss or a postponement is first stated (Indofood Agri 2022 and 2024, Old Chang Kee 2022 and
  2024, Kim Heng 2023 and 2024). Report publication dates are not in the store yet.
